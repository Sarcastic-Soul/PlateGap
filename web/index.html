PlateGap — what your meal plan leaves out

Your meal plan or mess fee is already paid, so the food on the counter is free at the margin — but rationed. This works out how well you could possibly eat on it, what that still leaves you short of, and the cheapest thing you could buy with your own money to close the difference.

Nutrients come from USDA FoodData Central, SR Legacy. Dishes are computed from ingredient recipes in stated grams, not estimated whole — every assumption is in the repository and can be argued with. The optimiser is a two-phase simplex written for this project, with duals checked against SciPy on a few hundred random instances.
