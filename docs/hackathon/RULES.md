## Submission Requirements

To submit an eligible entry to the Hackathon, participants must:

* Connect a coding agent to the AWS console before or during the Submission Period;
* Build and ship a live application on AWS that is publicly reachable by judges and the AI scoring system;
* Choose one of five app categories: Workplace Efficiency, Daily Life Enhancement, Commercial Potential, Social Good, or Personal Expression;
* Choose a focus track: Community or Startup;
* Publish a project on AWS Builder Center that includes: (a) proof of coding agent connection, (b) description of the project and development process, (c) designation of app category and focus track, and (d) a link or URL to the live application;
* Create an original application that has not been previously published; and
* Document their use of AWS services and coding agent in their submission.

## Ship Gate (pass/fail)

Every Submission is subject to a mandatory ship gate verification. To qualify for judging, the project must:

* Be live on AWS and reachable via a public URL at the time of evaluation
* Include documented proof of coding agent connection to the AWS console
* Be accessible to both the AI scoring system and human judges

A project that is not live and reachable does not advance to judging, regardless of the quality of the idea. The ship gate is a pass/fail requirement with no exceptions.

## Evaluation Process

Following the close of the Submission Period on October 2, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. PT, AWS will evaluate all qualifying Submissions (those that pass the ship gate) through a two-gate judging process:

### Gate 1 — AI Scoring and Human Review (week of October 5. 2026)

All qualifying Submissions will be scored by a combination of AI and Human review on the following criteria:

* Technical Innovation & Originality (25%)
* Implementation Quality (25%)
* Community/Market Impact (25%)
* Creativity & Storytelling (25%)

The top 100 Submissions by composite score will advance to Gate 2.

### Gate 2 — Human Judging (week of Oct 12, 2026)

A panel of AWS experts and community leaders will evaluate the top 100 Submissions on:

* Technical Innovation & Originality (25%)
* Implementation Quality (25%)
* Community/Market Impact (25%)
* Creativity & Storytelling (25%)

The judging panel will select 5 winners across the app categories.
